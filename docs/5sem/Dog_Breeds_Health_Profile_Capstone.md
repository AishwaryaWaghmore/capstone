Dog Breeds Health Profile for Capstone Project

Pomeranian

Small toy breed (1.5–3.5 kg) with thick double coat.

Common Symptoms & Health Issues:

- Dental problems (bad breath, gum infection, tooth loss).

- Luxating patella (limping or skipping while walking).

- Tracheal collapse (dry cough, breathing difficulty).

- Hypoglycemia (weakness, shaking, low energy).

- Skin allergies (itching, hair loss).

Beagle

Medium-sized hound breed (9–11 kg) known for strong sense of smell.

Common Symptoms & Health Issues:

- Ear infections (head shaking, ear discharge).

- Obesity (weight gain, low stamina).

- Epilepsy (seizures, muscle twitching).

- Hypothyroidism (weight gain, hair thinning).

- Intervertebral disc disease (back pain, difficulty moving).

German Shepherd

Large working breed (22–40 kg) known for intelligence and loyalty.

Common Symptoms & Health Issues:

- Hip dysplasia (difficulty standing, limping).

- Elbow dysplasia (front leg lameness).

- Degenerative myelopathy (weakness in hind legs).

- Bloat (swollen abdomen, restlessness – emergency condition).

- Skin allergies (itching, redness).
